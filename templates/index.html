How This Was Built · This portfolio framework combines your financial profile with historical market data and forward-looking risk estimates to create a personalized investment recommendation. It uses advanced statistical analysis and machine learning to estimate potential return and risk, then constructs a diversified portfolio intended to align with your goals and personal risk tolerance. The portfolio is also tested across a wide range of simulated market conditions to better understand how it may perform over time.

This is intended to serve as an informed planning tool rather than a prediction of future results. A full technical explanation of the methodology which includes model design, assumptions, limitations, and portfolio construction details are available in the technical report (opens in new window).
